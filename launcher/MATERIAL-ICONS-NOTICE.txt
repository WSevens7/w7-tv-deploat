This launcher bundles 13 Google Material Icons Round vectors distributed with
Android SDK's icons/material/materialiconsround collection.

Source project: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons
License: Apache License 2.0; see MATERIAL-ICONS-LICENSE.txt.
Google LLC and Material Design contributors.

Icons: wifi, settings, settings_input_hdmi, tv, volume_up, schedule, bedtime,
close, apps, palette, brightness_6, settings_input_component, wallpaper.

Local changes: intrinsic display dimensions changed from 24dp to 22dp;
theme tint changed to white for use over glass. Original vector paths retained.
